# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Stack

React Native with Expo SDK 57 and a Node 24 API. The local build uses persistent SQLite; a server-side adapter supports Turso libSQL when configured.

## Users

Music fans who want to express and compare opinions with friends, especially through ranked songs and albums.

## Product Purpose

Make music taste something people can record, debate, remix, and share. A successful first session starts with a ranking and ends with a comparison or shareable result.

## Positioning

The primary social action is making your own order of someone else's music selection, then seeing where the rankings agree and diverge.

## Operating Context

Android phones are the primary surface. The app sits beside music services and does not stream music. Spotify supplies account connection, search, and playlist imports when configured; public catalog search provides a metadata fallback. Published posts and social data live on the API; drafts remain on the device.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Local version: accounts, profiles, music search, manual rankings, Battle Mode, device drafts, server publishing, following, reactions, comments, remixing, comparison, mood boards with photos/notes/music, in-app activity, privacy enforcement, blocking/reporting, moderation, and PNG sharing. Visitor examples are labeled. Live Turso, Spotify, and email delivery require owner configuration. Native device QA and public-release operations remain necessary.

## Brand Commitments

Original identity. Editorial, culture-driven, music-focused, premium and confident. Avoid copying existing ranking apps, streaming apps, or generic social feeds. Music artwork supplies much of the color.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied a detailed product brief in an attachment. No name, logo, licensing arrangement, account credentials, or backend exists yet.

## Product Principles

- Rankings are the core creative format.
- Different opinions create a friendly conversation.
- Profiles express taste before follower metrics.
- Sharing should produce designed artifacts.
- Private work remains useful and under the user's control.
